# LeadTrigger.ai — Product Vision

> *LeadTrigger.ai turns a seller's deep understanding of their own offering into a precision-guided, AI-powered prospecting machine — so sales teams spend every minute on the right company, at the right moment, with the right reason to call.*

---

## Tagline

> **Know who to call. Know why. Know when.**

---

## Vision Breakdown

### "A seller's deep understanding of their own offering"

The **Offering Brain** is the beating heart of the system. Everything downstream — fit scoring, niche clustering, outreach generation — is anchored to a structured, AI-researched model of what the seller actually does and who they serve. The tool does not work without this foundation.

### "Precision-guided"

The **three-tier funnel** (Fit → Noise → Deep Research) is explicitly designed to avoid wasting effort. Companies are only researched deeply when they have passed both a fit gate and a trigger event gate. This is a deliberate architectural choice, not just a feature.

### "AI-powered prospecting machine"

The system automates the most time-consuming parts of prospecting: finding companies, clustering them into niches, scoring fit, detecting buying signals, generating briefings, and drafting outreach. The human stays in the loop for judgment — not for grunt work.

### "Right company, right moment, right reason"

This is the **three-variable problem** the tool solves:

| Variable | System Component |
|---|---|
| Right company | Fit score (Offering Brain × Apollo data) |
| Right moment | Noise detection — trigger events and buying signals |
| Right reason | Conversation starter + Deep Research brief |

All three must align before a rep picks up the phone.

---

## Architectural Principles

These principles are derived from the vision and should guide every product decision:

1. **Offering-first.** No feature should bypass the Offering Brain. Fit scoring, niche clustering, and outreach generation are all downstream of the offering model.
2. **Gate before you research.** Deep research (brief generation, sales approach) is expensive. Only companies that pass the fit gate and the noise gate earn it.
3. **Human judgment, not human grunt work.** Automate discovery, enrichment, clustering, scoring, briefing, and outreach drafting. Reserve human attention for decisions: call or skip, pursue or nurture.
4. **Precision over volume.** A list of 10 high-fit, high-urgency companies with a reason to call is worth more than 1,000 cold contacts.
5. **Structured intelligence.** Every output — brief, approach, niche label — should be traceable to a source. Confidence without evidence is noise.

---

## The Three-Variable Problem (Expanded)

A sales rep needs three things to make a confident, effective call:

- **Who** — a company that genuinely fits the offering (industry, size, pain profile, buying capacity)
- **When** — a moment when the company is likely to be receptive (expansion, leadership change, funding, product launch, regulatory pressure)
- **Why** — a specific, credible reason to reach out that connects the offering to the company's current situation

LeadTrigger.ai is built to solve all three simultaneously, at scale, without requiring the rep to spend hours on manual research.
